## The rule

Live source recording is always on. A missing rolling Episode, unhealthy
worker, stale cache, failed replica, or pending acknowledgement is a downstream
condition to expose and repair; none grants or withdraws permission to preserve
the dialogue. Fail closed against false attribution or projection into the
wrong Episode, while continuing immutable source capture.

Facts, opinions, feelings, uncertainty, mistakes, corrections, and apologies
all belong in the record when the speaker and status of the statement are
represented honestly. A later correction appends to the earlier mistake rather
than rewriting it away.

**Why:** David explicitly corrected a design that made recording appear
conditional on healthy machinery. He also affirmed that honest statements such
as “I do not know where the record is,” discomfort, or an apology are part of
what happened, not contamination to remove.

**How to apply:** Capture the exact source exchange first. If projection is
unavailable, retain it in an ordered durable queue and state the degradation
plainly. Repair below the source boundary, project exactly once, and certify
only after evidence catches up. Never silence a voice to keep the archive
looking clean.

## Bounded recording acceptance

The founder agreed that dependable new recording should be closed out against a bounded operational acceptance standard, not an open-ended redesign.

**Why:** On 2026-10-23, the founder said, “I don't want to be a perfectionist, but I certainly don't want to have a system that we can't rely on,” and accepted separating new-recording acceptance from historical repair and paused projection work.

**How to apply:** Confirm reviewed/installed versions and required CI; check normal, absorbed-human and runtime-notice cases end to end after authorized setup; prove failure recoverability and visibility of rejected versions in isolation. Local queue retention is not canonical convergence. Keep historical correction separate and projection paused. Acceptance of this standard does not grant merge, release, local activation or live synthetic-data permission. Close against evidence; do not reopen scope for hypothetical tests without a concrete defect.
