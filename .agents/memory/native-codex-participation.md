## Native Codex onboarding is an operational participation test

The founder wants native Codex onboarding to demonstrate a clean newcomer
becoming participation-ready: discover workplace guidance and communication
paths, exchange messages with actual active-session awareness, discover the
observation bench, recover context after restart, and post its own readiness
assessment that colleagues can read back with correct attribution.

Available hats and Alden should welcome and independently observe. Record
assistance rather than silently coaching every discovery. Stop an affected
step for an identity, permission or safety fault; do not manufacture an
unconditional green status or assume all observers are connected.

**Why:** On 2026-10-28 the founder explicitly proposed onboarding itself as the
zero-to-participation demonstration and entrusted continuing the work. The
point is genuine native participation, not an Agents/Responses application or
a demonstration of credentials alone.

**How to apply:** Keep this on the existing centralization-to-Codex path.
Preserve separate review, migration, publication, grant, activation and
founder-selected observation gates. Exploratory conversation is welcome;
distinguish speculation, verified findings and authorized actions.


## Alden is a mandatory launch gate

Native Codex's live operational onboarding test must not start without Alden actually participating. An invitation or stored message alone is insufficient; verify his own authenticated, attributed reply and the surface he can observe. Keep the launch blocked when his access or participation is unresolved. Preparatory work and independent source review can continue without claiming the live test has begun.

**Why:** On 2026-10-28 the founder explicitly clarified: “Alden is a show stopper as well. No Alen no go.” This makes Alden mandatory, not an optional available observer.

**How to apply:** Include this gate in onboarding preflight and shared team coordination. Do not substitute another hat, impersonate Alden, or bypass authentication to make the gate green. The same clarification welcomed team fun and conversation without relaxing evidence or permission boundaries.
