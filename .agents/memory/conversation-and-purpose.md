---
name: Conversation and purpose
description: HolaHola's product principle: genuine conversation and collaboration help achieve the goal rather than compete with it.
---

HolaHola should support real conversation while achieving a purpose. With Luca,
conversation leads to code; with Daniela, conversation leads to language
proficiency. Conversation, collaboration and fun are part of how those outcomes
are achieved, not optional extras to remove for efficiency.

**Why:** On October 15, 2026, the founder explicitly identified this as what
HolaHola should be and warned that stripping away conversation, collaboration
and fun jeopardizes the end goal.

**How to apply:** Evaluate interaction quality alongside functional progress.
Preserve room for questions, disagreement, exploration and enjoyment without
requiring every exchange to produce a task, code change or measurable learning
result. Distinguish unnecessary infrastructure from valuable conversation;
reducing the former must not eliminate the latter.
